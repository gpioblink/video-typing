Video Typing original C109 demonstration media
=============================================

Files: first-steps.webm, a-quiet-morning.webm

These are original procedural geometric animations and original practice
sentences created for Video Typing. They contain no third-party videos,
lyrics, subtitles, photographs, music, fonts, or prerecorded human voices.

To the extent copyright or related rights apply, the authors dedicate the
original animation, practice text, and generated recordings to the public
domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Narration is synthesized locally with the default English formant voice of
eSpeak NG (or eSpeak); it is not an impersonation of a particular person.
eSpeak NG: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng (GPL-3.0-or-later).
Encoding uses FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org/.
These tools are not bundled with the application. Their software licences
remain unchanged; this dedication concerns the original generated media.

Rebuild with: python3 new/scripts/generate-demo-media.py
Requires Python 3, FFmpeg with VP9/Opus, and eSpeak NG or eSpeak.
Each clip lasts 40 seconds. Four cues begin at 0, 10, 20, and 30 seconds;
the corresponding narration begins 0.5 seconds into each cue. There are no
burned-in answers. Encoding can differ between tool versions.

first-steps:
  Hello world.
  Listen and type.
  Small steps count.
  You did it.

a-quiet-morning:
  The sun is warm.
  A bird sings.
  Take a deep breath.
  Today is a new day.
