Timing and catalogue provenance
------------------------------
Verified 2026-11-01 using the exact official YouTube uploads above in a
fresh Chrome session: both embeds played successfully, with durations of
888.061 seconds (Sintel) and 734.181 seconds (Tears of Steel).
Native YouTube captions were compared with the distributed SRT:
Sintel cues 1, 7, and 12 were displayed at 108.23, 130.48, and 154.72
seconds respectively, all within their published SRT time ranges.
Tears of Steel cues 24, 29, and 32 were displayed at 224.99, 237.93, and
246.73 seconds respectively, all within their published SRT time ranges.
The catalogue therefore uses zero added subtitle offset for these uploads.
Other uploads or new editions may require a different offset.

Japanese-track verification (2026-11-01): the exact same official YouTube
embeds were played with Japanese native captions selected (language ja).
Sintel Japanese cues 1, 7, and 12 were displayed at 108.65, 130.79, and
154.83 seconds. Tears of Steel Japanese cues 24, 29, and 32 were displayed
at 225.15, 238.03, and 246.82 seconds. Each was inside the corresponding
official Japanese SRT time range. This independently confirms offset 0
for the selected Japanese practice intervals.

The application selects practice ranges at runtime. It does not rewrite
the source subtitle text or represent this film dialogue as original text.
